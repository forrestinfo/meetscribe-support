Quick Help

  • Grant microphone permission to capture your own voice.
  • Grant Screen Recording permission to capture meeting audio from macOS apps.
  • Restart the app after granting Screen Recording permission.
  • Use local providers by default, or configure cloud providers manually in Settings.

Common Questions

  • Why can’t I hear remote participants in transcripts? Remote meeting audio is treated as system audio on macOS, so Screen Recording permission and system-audio capture must be enabled.
  • Does MeetScribe upload my data by default? No. MeetScribe is local-first. Cloud or local network providers must be chosen explicitly by the user.
  • Why is system translation unavailable? Some language pairs rely on Apple system translation packages or a user-configured translation provider.
