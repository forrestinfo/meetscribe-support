MeetScribe Privacy Policy

MeetScribe is designed as a local-first macOS and iPhone app for meeting transcription, translation, captions, and notes.

What MeetScribe Accesses

  • Microphone and speech recognition: only after the user starts a meeting, to capture and transcribe sound reaching the device microphone.
  • System Audio Recording Only on macOS: when the user starts a meeting, the macOS app can use Apple's Core Audio Process Tap to receive audio samples played by meeting apps. The iPhone app does not capture audio from other apps. MeetScribe has no feature that records the screen and does not collect pixels, screenshots, video, window titles, window contents, or screen metadata.
  • Calendar: to read nearby event titles and suggest a meeting name. MeetScribe does not create, modify, or delete calendar events.
  • User-selected files and folders on macOS: before a recording can start, the user chooses a visible save folder with the standard macOS folder-selection dialog. The user may also explicitly select a local audio or video file for transcription. MeetScribe copies that file into the visible meeting folder and, for video, decodes only the audio track without reading, rendering, analyzing, or uploading video frames.
  • Protected local app storage on iPhone: meeting audio, transcripts, translations, and notes remain in the app's protected local storage until the user exports or deletes them.

Default Data Handling

  • Audio, transcripts, translations, and notes are stored locally: in the user-selected visible folder on macOS and in protected app storage on iPhone.
  • MeetScribe does not upload meeting audio or transcript content by default.
  • Credentials for optional hosted services are stored in the platform Keychain.
  • Users control where exported records and summaries are saved.

Optional Third-Party Providers

MeetScribe can connect to user-configured local or cloud providers for speech recognition, translation, or summary generation. Audio samples are sent only to a speech provider the user explicitly configures and selects. Transcript text is sent only to a translation or summary provider the user explicitly configures and selects. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and terms. MeetScribe's developer does not receive a copy.

Analytics and Tracking

  • No third-party advertising SDKs are included.
  • No tracking is performed by default.
  • No personal meeting data is collected for analytics by default.

Retention and Deletion

MeetScribe does not currently operate a developer-controlled cloud database for meeting content. On macOS, local records remain until the user deletes them from the selected folder. On iPhone, users can delete completed meetings inside MeetScribe and temporary exports are removed after sharing. Credentials for optional hosted services can be removed from MeetScribe and the platform Keychain. Third-party provider retention is governed by the provider selected by the user.

Data Sharing

MeetScribe does not sell personal data. Meeting content is shared only with a provider explicitly configured by the user when needed to fulfill a requested speech-recognition, translation, or summary operation.
